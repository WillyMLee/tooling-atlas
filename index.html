03 / Rules of use

Good diagrams earn their space.

  1. 01Name the relationship first.

    Sequence, hierarchy, comparison, causality, and spatial position need different structures.

  2. 02Keep prose outside the geometry.

    The shape should clarify the model; the surrounding copy should hold nuance and evidence.

  3. 03Recompose on mobile.

    Switch from spatial layout to a readable stack instead of shrinking labels below comprehension.

  4. 04Make the static state complete.

    Motion may direct attention, but the relationship must remain legible without it.

# Website pattern audit

Initial inventory recorded August 15, 2026 from eleven Cloudflare Pages projects.

## Pattern families

### Editorial systems

Observed in Signal Notes, Mumblings, Cost Basis, and Margins and Weather.

Useful ideas:

- numbered top-level views;
- large editorial headline paired with a structured archive;
- a distinct writing surface separated from the reading experience;
- warm paper or technical grid as a strong but restrained identity layer.

Recurring risk: templates can become visually interchangeable if typography, spacing, and navigation are copied as one bundle.

### Technical workbenches

Observed in MotionDeck Studio and SoundDeck.

Useful ideas:

- persistent asset taxonomy;
- task modes that reflect the production workflow;
- live previews beside parameter controls;
- status and provenance attached to each output.

Recurring risk: hundreds of controls and cards require progressive disclosure and reliable defaults.

### Living workbooks

Observed in Fleeting and Kaggriculture Fieldbook.

Useful ideas:

- documentation and runnable prototypes in one place;
- numbered chapters that turn an unfinished project into a navigable system;
- explicit experiment state and findings;
- roadmaps anchored to evidence rather than feature lists.

Recurring risk: the workbook can explain everything while failing to identify the next action.

### Games and data products

Observed in Pokémon Rogue Dungeons and Worth More?.

Useful ideas:

- a simple interaction loop as the front door to deeper information;
- insights generated from play or use;
- a separate data studio for advanced inspection.

Recurring risk: analytical surfaces interrupt the primary loop.

### Spatial discovery

Observed in Good Places, Together.

Useful ideas:

- map position as primary navigation;
- editorial cards that explain why a place matters;
- filters that preserve a point of view rather than imitate a generic directory.

Recurring risk: maps, filters, and narrative compete for a small mobile viewport.

## Extraction policy

Tooling Atlas records relationships, design decisions, and newly written implementation patterns. It does not publish private repository code, proprietary data, credentials, or copied brand assets.

The goal is a transferable pattern, not a template clone.

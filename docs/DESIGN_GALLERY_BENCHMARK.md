# Design gallery benchmark

Reviewed 2026-08-17 against:

- [Refero](https://refero.design/) and [Refero Styles](https://styles.refero.design/)
- [Hoverstat.es](https://www.hoverstat.es/) and its [archive](https://www.hoverstat.es/archive/)
- [Curated website inspiration](https://craftwork.design/curated/websites)

## What a useful gallery must answer

1. **Can I find the right reference?** Search by task, archetype, theme, component, or interaction—not only by project name.
2. **Can I understand why it matters?** Every item needs a short editorial explanation of the decision worth studying.
3. **Can I see the whole and the parts?** Preserve an opening frame, show real below-fold or alternate product views, then isolate component-level crops.
4. **Can I reconstruct it?** Record dimensions, hierarchy, components, breakpoints, source files, and an ordered build brief.
5. **Can I verify provenance?** Link the live source and label capture date, viewport, and coverage limits.

## Patterns retained

| Reference | Useful pattern | Atlas response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Refero | Separate screens, flows, UX patterns, UI elements, and visual styles by research intent. | Searchable design families plus distinct page-frame, component, responsive, and build-spec layers. |
| Hoverstat.es | Pair a large visual reference with a concise explanation of the unusual or reusable move. | Every profile begins with a use / study / avoid research brief. |
| Curated | Switch between whole websites and reusable sections; keep the original site action close to the reference. | Three live page or product-state captures precede the opening-frame component crops, with a persistent source link. |

## Atlas quality gate

A design profile is complete only when it has:

- one verified desktop opening frame;
- two additional live page frames or distinct product views;
- three opening-frame component crops: navigation, primary hero, and supporting module;
- a named archetype, layout, breakpoint, component list, page sequence, build order, and source provenance;
- no claim that a specified section has screenshot coverage when it does not.

The gallery is intentionally smaller than the benchmark libraries. Its advantage should be depth and agent-ready reconstruction, not volume.
